# Presentation illustrations

Downloaded on October 5, 2026. Exact URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in `sources.json`.
Images are included unchanged; LaTeX scales them for display.

- `illustrations/gemma-4.png`: Google, *Gemma 4: Our most capable open models to date*,
  https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemma-4/ .
  Used as model branding, with a credit on the final reference slide. The original
  artwork remains Google's; no open-content license is asserted.
- `illustrations/nvidia-l4.jpg`: NVIDIA, *NVIDIA L4 Tensor Core GPU*,
  https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/l4/ . Used to illustrate the hardware,
  with a credit on the final reference slide. The original artwork remains NVIDIA's;
  no open-content license is asserted.
- `emoji/*.png`: Twitter Twemoji v14.0.2 graphics, CC BY 4.0,
  https://github.com/twitter/twemoji/tree/v14.0.2 . Robot (U+1F916), wrench (U+1F527),
  microscope (U+1F52C), and rocket (U+1F680). Unmodified 72-pixel PNGs; license in
  `emoji/LICENSE-GRAPHICS`. Credit is also on the final reference slide.
- University logo provenance is in `logos/README.md`.

Illustrations and emoji are bundled locally for reproducible offline compilation
with both XeLaTeX and pdfLaTeX. They require no emoji font or network fetch at build time.
